Storm Damage Preparation Checklist in New Bedford, MA

Home / Blog / Storm Damage Preparation Checklist in New Bedford, MA

In New Bedford, MA, a storm can leave homes and businesses with devastating water damage. Getting ready ahead of the season, and again ahead of each storm, can significantly cut both the damage to your property and what restoration costs later. Use this New Bedford Restoration checklist to see what preparing a New Bedford home involves from start to finish.

Getting Ready Before Storm Season

Complete these tasks before storm season begins: Inspect your roof for damaged, missing, or worn shingles and flashing. Clear out every gutter and downspout, then confirm water discharges the way it should. Go around all exterior penetrations, doors, and windows, checking and sealing as you go. Inspect your sump pump and test the backup power system. Read through your coverage so you know where it stands on flood damage and wind damage. Take up-to-date photos of the property to keep with your insurance records. Branches driven by wind cause less harm when nearby trees have been trimmed.

Just Before a Storm Arrives

With a storm forecast, first secure anything loose outside, since it could become a projectile. If conditions call for it, board up the windows or close your storm shutters. Confirm that the sump pump runs and that its backup battery is charged. Move valuables and important documents to upper levels or waterproof containers. Learn the way out if you must evacuate, and keep your supplies packed. Save the New Bedford Restoration emergency line, (915) 288-9916, in your phone contacts.

After the Storm Ends

After the storm passes: Check for damage from a safe distance before entering. Record every bit of damage on video and in photos. If you can do it safely, tarp over any openings in the roof. Electrical hazards must be assessed before anyone steps into a flooded area. Contact your insurance company to report damage.
